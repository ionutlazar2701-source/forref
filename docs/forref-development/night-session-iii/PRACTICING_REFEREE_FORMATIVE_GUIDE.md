# Practicing referee formative guide

Status of this file: RECORD

Run this only after the Founder diagnostic pilot has not left an open P0.

## Sample

About 8 to 12 practicing referees. This is formative. It is not a statistical certification sample and it is not phone-authority acceptance.

Record, when the referee offers it: years of practice, usual competition level, age band, and how often they use a phone for match administration. Do not require an answer.

The Founder is not one of these seats.

## Session shape

One referee, one moderator, the research prototype, and the sheet from the protocol.

Same launch rule: the bar says `FORREF RESEARCH PROTOTYPE`.

Tasks use the same scenario ids as the Founder pilot, in the same order, unless a P0 already retired a task. Add `s11SubstitutionOutFirst` and `s13SubstitutionInterruptAfterFirst` when substitution is in that referee's block.

Do not teach the correct player after a miss until the first-attempt score is written down.

## What a pass of this study is not

A calm session does not authorize production engineering.

A confusing session does not change Match Core. It changes the instrument or the task wording, then the study can be repeated.

## Measures

Use the protocol list. First-attempt subject and eventual completion are both required on every identity task.

Confidence is the referee's own sentence, not the moderator's grade.
