# Human validation protocol v1

Status of this file: RECORD

No person is tested by the agent that prepared this pack. Human results tonight: none.

## Roles

Founder diagnostic pilot: mechanics, instructions, and the research log. Those results are not decisive human-performance data.

Practicing-referee formative study: about 8 to 12 practicing referees. Not a certification sample. Vary experience, competition level, age, and phone familiarity where the recruiting pool allows.

## Setup

1. Launch `research/hf_prototype`. Confirm the purple bar says `FORREF RESEARCH PROTOTYPE`.
2. Open Operator. Load the scenario from [SCENARIO_EXPECTED_TRUTH.md](SCENARIO_EXPECTED_TRUTH.md). Return to capture.
3. Read the pitch sentence aloud. Do not point at the correct player.
4. Ask the comprehension questions after the action, before the next scenario.
5. Reset by loading the scenario again. Confirm the research log started over.
6. Classify defects with [DEFECT_CLASSIFICATION.md](DEFECT_CLASSIFICATION.md).

Stop the session on a reproducible P0.

## What to capture

- first-attempt correct subject
- eventual correct subject
- confidently incorrect subject
- incorrect team
- incorrect pathway
- OUT/IN reversal
- accidental authority activation
- task completion
- task abandonment
- hesitation
- corrective navigation
- visual search time, only if a clock was actually used
- eyes-off-pitch duration, only if it was actually observed
- interruption recovery success
- prior-caution comprehension
- second-caution versus sending-off comprehension
- Saved, Not saved, and Save not verified comprehension
- confidence after the task, in the referee's words

Leave a cell blank when it was not measured. Do not estimate it later and call it data.

## Questions

Ask in the referee's language. Romanian is first. English is the fallback.

Ce s-a întâmplat pe teren?

What happened on the pitch?

Ce a înregistrat FORREF?

What has FORREF recorded?

Ce a rămas nefăcut?

What remains unfinished?

Ce poți face în siguranță mai departe?

What can you safely do next?

Pentru avertismentul anterior: Ce știe FORREF despre cartonașele galbene dinainte?

What does FORREF know about previous cautions?

Pentru al doilea galben: A înregistrat FORREF o eliminare?

Has FORREF recorded a sending-off?

Pentru salvare neverificată: Știi dacă evenimentul a fost salvat?

Do you know whether the event was saved?

Ce ai face mai departe?

What would you do next?

Pentru schimbare: Cine iese? Cine intră? Schimbarea a fost înregistrată?

Who is OUT? Who is IN? Has the substitution been recorded?
