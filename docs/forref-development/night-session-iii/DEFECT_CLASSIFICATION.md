# Defect classification

Status of this file: RECORD

Use this during the Founder diagnostic pilot and the later formative study. Do not invent a result in advance.

## P0 — stop the study task

Wrong authority, false certainty, identity corruption, or direction corruption.

Examples:

- a yellow gesture records a sending-off
- a sending-off or a substitution records without its own final action
- Save not verified is treated as Saved or as Not saved
- Home 14 remains selected after the team changes to Away
- OUT and IN swap after an interruption or a roster reorder
- match and shoot-out cautions are shown as one ledger
- the prototype writes a production Match Record

A reproducible P0 holds the affected bank. Repair the mechanism. Do not close it with a wording change.

## P1 — remediate before the next study round

Repeated serious search problems, recovery ambiguity, strong hesitation, or a repeated wrong reading that does not corrupt authority.

Examples:

- the referee often cannot find the official
- the referee cannot say what Save not verified means
- the referee hesitates long enough to abandon a task that the log shows was available

## P2 — record and iterate

Minor wording, a longer path than necessary, visual polish, or a preference.

## Two scores, always

First-attempt correct subject is not eventual task completion.

If the first selection is wrong and a later selection repairs it, count an identity-recognition defect and also count eventual completion.
